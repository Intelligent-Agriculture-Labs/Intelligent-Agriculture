The solution

One simple app, deep science underneath

This week — at most three actions: irrigate how much and when, drought, disease, pests, frost and heat. Each has a plain-language Why? with the NASA evidence.

My fields — tap a field on sub-metre imagery and AI draws its boundary; you can edit it. Sentinel-2 history, AI management zones, erosion, wetness and frost maps.

Plan — multi-year rotation plan per field, checked against cited agronomy, with an optimised alternative and its impact.

Everywhere — accounts that sync phone and PC, offline mode, SMS-length summaries, 6 languages.

Field Shift: plan the next years

Every field gets a rotation that fits its biology and climate

The farmer enters past and planned crops. IA checks each year: disease and pest carry-over, nitrogen from legumes, bare soil and erosion, water need — and says what to change.

A beam-search optimiser proposes a better plan within the region's cropping system, and simulates both on the field's own NASA weather.

Does it work?

Validated, weak spots included

{{VALIDATION}}

Reported openly in VALIDATION.md and LIMITATIONS.md:

  • Soil-water dynamics weaker in Kenya than in Iowa.
  • Drought status agrees less in irrigated Nevada.
  • Field detection struggles on terraced plots — the outline is always editable.
  • Impact numbers are model estimates, not yet measured on farms.
