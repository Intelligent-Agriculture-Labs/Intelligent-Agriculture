NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data

FieldShift

40 years of NASA Earth observations, turned into this week's farm decisions and a science-based crop-rotation plan for every field, in plain language and six languages. Works on web, iOS and Android, online or offline.

What a farmer gets

Simple on the surface, deep underneath: every number is one tap from its NASA evidence.

This week

At most three actions: when and how much to irrigate, drought status, disease and pest risk, frost and heat — each with a plain "Why?".

Your field from space

Tap your field and AI finds its boundary. 10 m Sentinel-2 greenness history, cuttings seen from space, AI management zones, terrain maps of erosion, wetness and frost.

Plan the next years

A multi-year rotation plan per field, checked against 30 soil-borne diseases and pests, nitrogen, soil cover and water — with an AI-optimised alternative and its impact.

Everywhere

Accounts that sync phone and PC, offline mode, SMS-length summaries, and six languages incl. Swahili and Hindi.

Run it yourself

One command, no account, works offline with the bundled NASA demo data.

git clone {{REPO_URL}} && cd fieldshift && FIELDSHIFT_OFFLINE=1 docker compose up --build → open http://localhost:8080

FieldShift · Apache-2.0 · Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. Data: NASA POWER, SMAP, MODIS, ECOSTRESS, SRTM, GIBS; Copernicus Sentinel-2 & ERA5; ISRIC SoilGrids; Open-Meteo; U.S. Drought Monitor. Imagery: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics. Use of SI (AI) tools is disclosed in the repository README. FieldShift supports, and does not replace, farmer judgement and local advice.
